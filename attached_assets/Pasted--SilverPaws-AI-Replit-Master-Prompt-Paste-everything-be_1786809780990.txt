## Project Overview

I'm building **SilverPaws AI**, a pet health and gait analysis application for the Congressional App Challenge (CAC). The app analyzes video of a pet walking to detect gait abnormalities (limping, stiffness, uneven weight distribution) that could indicate joint pain, arthritis, or injury — helping pet owners catch mobility issues early and share objective data with their vet.

Team: Abhikshit, Sudarshan, and Kavin, with a technical build plan authored by a collaborator (Abhi).

## Core Technical Approach

**Do not train a custom pose model.** Use a pretrained model:
- **Pose/gait analysis:** MoveNet Thunder, loaded from TensorFlow Hub, run on video frames to extract keypoints over time. Gait metrics (stride symmetry, joint angle variance, movement smoothness) are derived from the keypoint sequence.
- **Backend:** FastAPI (Python), serving endpoints for video upload, running inference, and returning gait analysis results.
- **Database:** Firebase / Firestore for storing pet profiles, uploaded video metadata, and gait analysis history.
- **Reporting:** ReportLab to generate PDF summaries of a pet's gait analysis (e.g., to share with a vet).
- **Data handling:** OpenCV for video frame extraction/preprocessing, NumPy/pandas for gait metric computation.
- **Frontend:** Intentionally deferred until the AI pipeline and backend are validated. When built, it will be Flutter or React Native — do not scaffold frontend code yet unless explicitly asked.
